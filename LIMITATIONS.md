# Limitations (read before believing any number)

1. **No mule networks in the ČNB data.** All cross-bank results come from PLANTED red-team data. Detection rates measure how well we recognise our own scheme definitions; they say nothing about real-world recall or precision.
2. **The red-team generator and the detectors were written by the same team.** The persona separators (E6–E8) were designed after seeing false positives on our own personas; their false-positive rate on real customers is unknown. A mule who imitates an ordinary spending life would defeat them.
3. **Tuning on dev seeds.** D6 thresholds and the radar candidate band were chosen on dev seed 7; seeds 101, 202, 303, 404 were also looked at (sweeps, audit). Headline numbers use held-out seeds 505 and 606 (see `eval/report.md`); the pipeline was not changed after they were generated except for a timestamp clamp in the generator that only affects events past 31 July. Two held-out worlds are a small sample: on seed 505 the radar adds nothing at priority 30 (63/9/0 in both modes); on 606 it adds 3 true mules. The mean benefit (F1 0.925 -> 0.938) is small and noisy.
4. **Rule A is noise at CNB scale** (60–99.9 % of clients under every unit interpretation). The value is in triage, not in the rule.
5. **`naive_raw_sharing` is not a guaranteed upper bound**: it pools raw data into one node with thresholds calibrated per bank.
6. **PSI is semi-honest DH-PSI** (256-bit secret exponents on the RFC 3526 2048-bit group; subgroup check by Jacobi symbol on every element that meets our exponent). Measured on this 12-core laptop: 1k tokens per bank 14 s (mostly process-pool start-up on Windows), 10k 23 s, 100k 194 s, 102 MB on the wire at 100k; a single-core machine would be roughly 10x slower. Probing and token poisoning are mitigated by guards and humans-in-the-loop, not eliminated. No Clean Rooms / Nitro Enclaves are used. **PSI is not a legal basis**: sharing needs the banking-secrecy change (§ 38 Act 21/1992; planned Safe-D) and, under EU AMLR Art. 75 (from 10 July 2027), a notified partnership with a DPIA.
7. **Placeholders:** FX rates, escalation matrix, bank settings (sender/contact), model prices (budget guard-rails only). The EU high-risk list was compiled from web search and not verified against the consolidated EUR-Lex text; FATF lists were read from the FATF page (19 June 2026 plenary).
8. **Out of scope for the data we have:** card, ATM, merchant, device and IP detectors (interfaces only). Behavioural/device vendors (ThreatMark, BioCatch) cover that part and are complementary.
9. **Deployment:** the demo API/UI run the three bank nodes in one process; a distributed mode (one process per bank, separate radar service, traffic-inspection tests) exists in `node/service.py`, `radar/service.py`, `scripts/run_local_cluster.py` but is not wired into the API/UI, `docker compose` was written and **not run**, and there is no TLS, service authentication, key store or key rotation. Agents (copilot) run in the API process, not per bank. The distributed services use the master-prompt cluster scoring and do not yet carry the weak-evidence significance statistics.
10. **LLM:** explanations are validated for supported numbers and references, not for semantic correctness (a model once described a retention *ratio* as a duration); a human reads every draft. Latency measured on 2026-10-19: Kimi-K3 ≈ 3 min per task, Claude Sonnet 4.5 ≈ 40–50 s. Costs shown use placeholder prices.
11. **IBM AMLworld (generic laundering patterns, not Czech mules): our engine is weak.** On held-out days 10-18 (1,304 positive accounts, prevalence 0.9 %) isolated and staj_psi both reach F1 0.043 / recall 0.19 / precision 0.024; average precision is 0.037 isolated vs 0.051 with the radar (a ranking improvement, no new flags); a one-line distinct-counterparties ranking beats the engine (AP 0.041). The engine misses pure senders and pure receivers of fan-out/fan-in patterns. `naive_raw_sharing` is again not an upper bound. Held-out window is mostly one day. See `docs/IBM_EVAL.md` and `eval/out/ibm_eval.json`.
12. **Weak-evidence radar (pre-registered experiment).** On held-out seeds 707/808 the radar finds the planted `ghost_ring` (accounts with no usable local signal) 0 % -> 100 % of the time with no extra false positives, but: (a) the ghost ring is our own scenario, added after a first sweep showed the baseline already at ceiling on the original schemes; (b) the weak score assumes the ring forwards money within hours; (c) the result is fragile in the knob - at `weak_top_frac` 0.10 the ring is no longer found and at 0.20-0.40 original-scheme recall drops, because more exported candidates dilute the significance; (d) the privacy cost is real: ~6.6x more exported tokens, ~8x more bytes, ~4-5x more match rows seen by the coordinator, plus the bank-local degree of every MATCHED token; (e) it needs calibration on real data.
13. The OPO is a **draft**: sections VI (executed/not executed/postponed) and IX (submission) are left to a human; no analyst name appears in the body.
14. **Collector (pre-registered experiment, eval/collector_report.md).** Synthetic worlds only; one team writes generator and detectors. The planted cash-out account has no honest payers, which makes shared-hub detection easy; real hubs mix honest and mule payers. If the collector colludes with all but one bank, that bank's counts are revealed. A round needs all banks (no dropout recovery). The baseline has 30 days of history (cloned from the evaluation month). The existing radar (b) catches some stealth mules on some days of the month, so conclusions depend on whether a flag persists in the queue. HOW was frozen on 3 dev seeds (first dev run killed by a memory guard); its test-seed run is reported. The legal status of sharing counts about all customers is not confirmed.
